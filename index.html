The Sunshine Programme

A special place in our hearts for differently-abled children. The programme gives children with differences the chance to enjoy The Playhouse as a VIP.

Free membership

  • Life-long access to The Playhouse at no charge
  • An extra 25% off for 2 friends
  • Unlimited train rides
  • A priority card you can use on any trading day, unless we're closed for a private function
  • No booking needed

Sunshine Days

Days exclusively for members. No public allowed, so it's a calm, controlled space for children who are sensitive to crowds and loud noises.

  • Parents are allowed on the slides

How to apply

Children with Cerebral Palsy, Down Syndrome, Autism Spectrum, life-threatening illnesses, childhood cancers and more are welcome to apply. Send us a WhatsApp and we'll guide you through the simple application.

Before you come

House rules

  • Socks are required for everyone in the playground. No shoes or bare feet.
  • The playground is unsupervised and there are no child minders. Adults must supervise their kids.
  • Adults are not allowed on the slides.
  • We are an alcohol-free facility.
  • No liquids, including water. Baby items are excluded.
  • No drinking or loitering in the car park.
  • No weapons, sharp objects or dangerous toys. Bags are searched on arrival.

How to book

  1. Open the booking page and pick a date. Greyed-out dates are closed.
  2. Choose the number of guests (kids and adults) and your arrival time.
  3. Pick your seating: general, or the Combo Deal (R349 per child with unlimited play, a meal combo and a lucky packet).
  4. Enter your details and accept the admission policy.
  5. Pay online. General admission is R150 for 90 minutes. You can upgrade to Unlimited Play on arrival and pay the difference.
  6. Check your email for the confirmation.
